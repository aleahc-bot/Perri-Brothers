Florida construction disclosures

Chapter 558, Florida Statutes, contains important requirements you must follow before you may bring a lawsuit or other action for an alleged construction defect. At least sixty (60) days before filing an action, you must deliver to us a written notice describing in reasonable detail each alleged construction defect and give us an opportunity to inspect the property and to offer to repair, offer monetary compensation, or dispute the claim.
