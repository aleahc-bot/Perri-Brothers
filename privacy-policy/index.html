Privacy Policy

Perri Brothers Construction (“COMPANY,” “we,” “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use perribrothers.com (the “Site”) or contact us about a project, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies alongside our Terms of Service.

SMS, Text Messaging & Mobile Information

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories of data exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

If you provide your mobile number and opt in to text messages, we use it only to send appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, rescheduling updates, and customer support messages relating to your project. Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Opt-in is collected through a dedicated SMS consent checkbox on our web forms. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and you can reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. Full messaging terms, including carrier liability and supported carriers, are set out in our SMS Messaging Terms & Compliance.

We share mobile numbers with our messaging service provider only to the extent necessary to deliver these messages on our behalf. That provider is contractually restricted to processing the data for that purpose and may not use it for its own marketing.

Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, phone number, email address, and the city or area your project is in
  • Details about the work you are considering, including anything you type into our contact or quote forms
  • Any information you send us by email, text message, or over the phone

Information collected automatically

  • Standard server and log information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the pages you view
  • Basic usage information needed to keep the Site working and secure

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule a consultation
  • To prepare estimates, scopes of work, and project communications
  • To send appointment confirmations and reminders, where you have opted in
  • To provide customer support and answer questions about work in progress
  • To maintain business records and comply with legal and tax obligations
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site

We do not sell your personal information.

Service Providers We Use

We rely on a small number of third-party providers to run the Site and manage enquiries. They process information on our behalf and only for the purposes we specify:

  • LeadConnector / HighLevel — hosts the contact form embedded on our Contact page and stores submissions in our customer relationship management system
  • Our website host — serves the Site and keeps standard server logs
  • Google Fonts — delivers the typefaces used on the Site, which involves your browser making a request to Google’s servers

We may also disclose information where we are required to do so by law, to enforce our Terms of Service, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our company, our clients, or others.

Cookies

The Site itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party components, such as the embedded contact form, may set cookies necessary for their own operation. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings, though doing so may affect how parts of the Site behave.

Data Retention

We keep enquiry and project information for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal, accounting, and record-keeping obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

Your Choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time
  • Email: ask us to stop sending non-essential email by contacting us
  • Access, correction, or deletion: request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Children’s Privacy

The Site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. You must be 18 years or older to participate in our SMS program. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Links

The Site may link to websites we do not operate, including our social media profiles. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page with its effective date. Continuing to use the Site after we post changes means you accept the updated policy.
