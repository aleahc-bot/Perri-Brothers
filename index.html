02 / About

Two Brothers. One Team. Our Name Is on Every Project.

We’re not a franchise, and we don’t hand projects off to someone you’ve never met. The people who meet with you are the same people who oversee your project from start to finish.

The owners

You will meet us on day one, and every day after.

Most homeowners meet with a salesperson, sign a contract, and rarely see that person again. We built Perri Brothers differently. When you meet with us, you’re meeting the people who will oversee your project from the initial walkthrough to the final punch list.

Because we’re personally involved in every job, we intentionally limit the number of projects we take on at one time. That allows us to stay accessible, maintain quality, and give every client the attention they deserve.

The Perri Difference

What the Perri Family Stands For.

Every project we take on is guided by the same principles: honest communication, uncompromising craftsmanship, and treating every home with the care we’d expect for our own.

I

Integrity

We believe honesty is the foundation of every successful project. If a simple repair is the right solution, we’ll tell you. If a larger renovation makes more sense, we’ll explain why. Our recommendations are always based on what’s best for you—not what’s most profitable for us.

II

Quality

Quality isn’t something we inspect at the end of a project—it’s built into every step. From material selection to craftsmanship, we focus on doing the job right the first time, without cutting corners.

III

Communication

IV

Accountability

We take responsibility for every project because our name is on every one. You’ll always know who’s leading your project and who to call when you have questions.

04 / Process

Five stages. No mystery months.

The hardest part of most renovations isn’t the construction, it’s not knowing what comes next. That’s why we guide you through every stage from start to finish.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We visit your home, learn about your goals, answer questions, and discuss realistic options before any plans are drawn.

    You leave with a realistic budget range.
  2. 02

    Design & Selections

    Choose layouts, finishes, fixtures, and materials together with our team using real samples—not just catalog photos.

    You leave with a finalized scope and transparent pricing.
  3. 03

    Permitting

    We coordinate the permitting process with local municipalities and keep you informed throughout. While approval timelines vary, we’ll communicate every update as it happens.

    You leave with approved permits and a scheduled start date.
  4. 04

    Build

    Our team manages every phase of construction with careful coordination, daily job site cleanup, and consistent communication from an owner throughout the project.

    You leave with regular updates and no unexpected change orders.
  5. 05

    Final Walkthrough

    We’ll walk through the completed project together, address any final details, and make sure everything meets your expectations before final payment.

    You leave with a completed punch list and written warranty information.
The honest part

Where projects actually go wrong.

Permitting

County review timelines can change, and while we can’t control municipal approvals, we’ll keep you informed every step of the way.

Selections

Making finish selections early helps keep your project moving and reduces the risk of scheduling delays.

Hidden Conditions

Older Southwest Florida homes can reveal hidden issues such as water damage, outdated wiring, or structural concerns. When we find them, we’ll explain your options clearly before moving forward.
