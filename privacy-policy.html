Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Perri Brothers Construction LLC (“Perri Brothers Construction,” “we,” “us,” “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit perribrothers.com, contact us, request an estimate, receive our construction or remodeling services, or exchange text messages with us.

We are a family-owned and operated general contractor serving Southwest Florida — in Lee County: Fort Myers, Fort Myers Beach, Cape Coral, Estero, Sanibel, and Lehigh Acres; and in Collier County: Naples and Bonita Springs.

By using our site or services you agree to this Policy and to our Terms & Conditions. If you do not agree, please do not use the site or submit information to us.

Information we collect

CategoryExamples
Identifiers & contact information The information you enter in our consultation request form — your name, phone number, email address, the city your project is in, the project type you select, and your description of what you want to accomplish — plus the mailing and project address you give us later.
Project information Description of the work you request, property details and access notes, plans, drawings, photos, and measurements you send us, appointment history, estimates, proposals, change orders, permit and inspection records, and notes from our crews and subcontractors.
Communications The content of your emails, web form submissions, voicemails, call notes, social media messages, and text message threads with us, including timestamps and delivery status.
Consent records Records of your opt-in to text messages or email marketing — including the date, time, method, IP address, and the exact disclosure language shown to you — and any later opt-out. We keep these to prove compliance with messaging and telemarketing rules, including the federal TCPA and the Florida Telephone Solicitation Act.
Payment information Billing name and address, invoice, deposit, draw, and payment history, and the last four digits and expiration of a card. Full card numbers and bank details are collected and stored by our payment processor, not by us.
Insurance & financing information For storm restoration or financed projects, the insurer or lender name, claim or application number, adjuster contact, and scope documents you choose to share with us.
Device & usage information IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, dates and times of access, and approximate location derived from IP.
Reviews & media Testimonials, reviews, and before/after project photos, where you provide them or consent to them.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories of personal information such as Social Security numbers, precise geolocation, biometric data, or health information. Please do not send those to us — especially not by text message. If financing requires sensitive information, provide it directly to the lender rather than to us.

How we collect it

  • Directly from you — web forms on perribrothers.com, phone calls, text messages, email, messages through our social media pages, in-person conversations, and signed work orders.
  • Automatically — cookies, pixels, and server logs when you browse our site.
  • From service providers — our scheduling, CRM, messaging, and payment vendors, which pass along the information you gave them on our behalf.
  • From third parties — lead and referral sources, review platforms, insurers and adjusters on restoration projects, architects and designers on your project team, and publicly available Lee and Collier County property records.

How we use your information

  • Respond to your inquiries and prepare estimates and proposals.
  • Schedule, perform, document, permit, inspect, and invoice the work you request.
  • Send you appointment confirmations, crew arrival windows, project status updates, and billing notices by text, email, or phone.
  • Provide customer support and handle warranty and punch-list claims.
  • Process payments and collect amounts owed, including lien and collection rights.
  • Keep records of your messaging consent and opt-outs.
  • Improve our website, services, and internal operations, including analytics.
  • Show completed work in our portfolio and on social media, where you have consented.
  • Send marketing communications, where you have opted in or where permitted by law, with an opt-out in every message.
  • Protect against fraud, secure our systems, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations, including contractor licensing and building-code recordkeeping.

Text messaging & mobile data

No mobile information — including your mobile phone number and your consent to receive text messages from Perri Brothers Construction — will be sold, rented, or shared with any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be shared only with the subcontracted vendors that help us operate our messaging program (for example our SMS platform and telecommunications providers), strictly so they can deliver messages on our behalf. All other categories of sharing described in When we share information exclude mobile opt-in data and SMS consent entirely.

What we collect for messaging

When you exchange texts with us we collect your mobile number, the content of the messages, the date and time each message was sent or received, delivery and error status returned by the carrier, and your opt-in and opt-out history.

How we use it

We use this information only to communicate with you about your request or project, to send the message types listed in our Terms & Conditions, to honor your opt-outs, to keep required consent records, and to troubleshoot delivery problems.

Consent and opting out

Text messages are sent only to numbers that have opted in. Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of purchasing our goods or services. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message; reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at perribrothersfl@gmail.com or (239) 910-0893. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. After you opt out we retain a record of your number on a suppression list — that record exists so we do not text you again, and it is not used to contact you.

What we cannot control

SMS and MMS are not end-to-end encrypted and travel over networks operated by wireless carriers. Carriers and messaging providers process message metadata under their own policies. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

When we share information

We share personal information only as described below, and — as stated above — never mobile opt-in data or SMS consent for anyone else's marketing.

  • Service providers. Vendors that work for us under contract: website hosting, CRM and scheduling, SMS and email platforms, telecommunications carriers, payment processing, accounting and bookkeeping, e-signature, cloud storage, and analytics. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • Subcontractors and suppliers. Where a project requires it, we share the project address, scope, and a contact name and phone number with the trade partners, material suppliers, cabinet and millwork shops, and delivery services performing or supplying that work.
  • Design professionals. Architects, engineers, and designers engaged on your project.
  • Permitting and inspection authorities. Information required by Lee and Collier County, municipal building departments, Sunshine 811 utility locates, and inspectors — including owner name, project address, scope, and valuation.
  • Financing partners. If you apply for project financing through a partner, the information you provide for that application.
  • Insurance carriers and adjusters. Where a claim, loss, or certificate of insurance requires it.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to establish or defend legal claims, perfect or enforce a construction lien under Chapter 713, Florida Statutes, enforce our Terms, prevent fraud, or protect the rights, property, or safety of any person.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, sale of assets, financing, or acquisition, subject to this Policy continuing to apply to the transferred information.
  • With your direction. Anyone you ask us to share with — for example a spouse, property manager, HOA or condominium association, tenant, or realtor.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising in exchange for payment. We have not sold or shared personal information for monetary or other valuable consideration in the preceding twelve (12) months, and we do not sell the personal information of anyone under 16.

Cookies & analytics

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use it. We may use analytics tools such as Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics to measure site traffic; you can opt out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the site — such as our contact and estimate forms — from working properly.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here. In general: project, contract, permit, and warranty records for at least seven (7) years after completion, to align with construction limitations and repose periods and our warranty obligations; invoices and payment records as required by tax law; text and email threads for three (3) years; consent and opt-out records for at least five (5) years, as recommended for messaging-compliance recordkeeping; and website analytics for fourteen (14) months. Suppression-list entries are kept indefinitely so that opt-outs are permanently honored. Project photographs may be retained in our portfolio indefinitely unless you ask us to remove them.

How we protect information

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to our size and the sensitivity of the information — including HTTPS on our website, access controls and least-privilege permissions, multi-factor authentication on business accounts, reputable vendors with their own security programs, and staff training. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and any required authorities as the law requires, including the Florida Department of Legal Affairs under the Florida Information Protection Act (§ 501.171, Florida Statutes).

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know and access the personal information we hold about you and how we use and disclose it.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Delete personal information, subject to exceptions such as records we must keep for tax, warranty, building-code, licensing, lien, safety, or legal-compliance reasons — including messaging consent and opt-out records.
  • Opt out of marketing emails and text messages at any time.
  • Portability — receive a copy of your information in a portable format.
  • Non-discrimination — we will not deny service, charge different prices, or provide a lower quality of service because you exercised a privacy right.

To make a request, email perribrothersfl@gmail.com or call (239) 910-0893 with your name, the address associated with your project, and the request you are making. We will verify your identity using information already in our records and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (generally 45 days, extendable once where permitted). An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with written authorization we can verify.

If we deny your request you may appeal by replying to our response with the word “Appeal.”

Florida residents

Florida's Telephone Solicitation Act restricts telephonic sales calls and text messages made without prior express written consent. We obtain that consent before sending any marketing text, keep a record of it, and honor every opt-out request promptly. Florida's Do Not Call program is administered by the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services; you may also register with the National Do Not Call Registry at donotcall.gov. Registering does not stop transactional messages about a project you have asked us to perform.

Consumer complaints about a Florida-licensed contractor may be directed to the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

Children's privacy

Our site and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and our messaging program is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. There is no common industry standard for responding to it, so our site does not currently respond to DNT signals. You can still use the cookie and advertising controls described above.

Third-party sites

Our site may link to third-party websites and tools we do not control, including review platforms, payment pages, and our social media pages. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, where the change is material, provide additional notice on our site. Please review this page periodically.
